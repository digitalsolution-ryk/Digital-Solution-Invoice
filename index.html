ℹ️ About Application

Digital Solutions Invoicing
Version: 2.0

A modern, easy-to-use invoicing and account-management app built for Digital Solutions — create invoices, track customers and payments, and keep a running ledger, all from your phone or computer.

Key Features

  • Dashboard with sales & outstanding balance overview
  • Professional invoices with logo, QR payment & Easypaisa details
  • Customer & service records with auto-fill
  • Payment status tracking (Paid / Unpaid / Partial)
  • Cash Receipt & Cash Payment vouchers
  • Customer Ledger (running account statement)
  • Daily / weekly / monthly reports
  • A4, A5 & thermal receipt print formats
  • Print, PDF download & WhatsApp share
  • Installable app, works offline (PWA)
